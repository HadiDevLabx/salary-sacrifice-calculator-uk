# Salary Sacrifice Calculator UK — What It Really Costs

> For a basic-rate taxpayer, £100 in the pension costs £80 by ordinary contribution but only £72 by salary sacrifice. The 8% difference is National Insurance you never paid.

Reference notes for the UK tax year **2026/27**, computed from HMRC's
published rates rather than copied from another site.

## How it works

Sacrifice comes off **before** tax and National Insurance are worked out, so the money was never your income.

| | Ordinary contribution | Salary sacrifice |
|---|---|---|
| Income Tax saved | yes | yes |
| Employee NI saved | no | **yes** |
| Employer NI saved (15%) | no | **yes** |
| Reduces gross for student loan | no | yes |

Many employers pass some of their own 15% saving into your pension. That money only exists on the sacrifice route, so it is worth asking.

## What this does not cover

Your reference salary falls, which can reduce mortgage borrowing, life cover, maternity pay and statutory redundancy pay. You cannot sacrifice below the National Minimum Wage. An announced £2,000 cap on NI relief for pension sacrifice starts in 2029.

## Source and tax year

Figures are for the 2026/27 tax year, which runs 6 April 2026
to 5 April 2027, and were checked against
[gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027) on 28 August 2026.

Rates change every April, and often at a Budget. Check the tax year a figure
belongs to before relying on it.
